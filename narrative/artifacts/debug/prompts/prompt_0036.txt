- BB: Block Billing | Lumping or aggregation of multiple tasks into a single narrative entry with a sum total of time instead of listing each task and the precise related time.
- CF: Churning File | Timekeeper repeatedly undertakes seemingly legitimate tasks which adds marginal value for client (e.g., repeated updates of corporate minute book, checking dockets, updating form contracts).

- CI: Communication Inspecificity | Outside counsel's time entries regarding electronic or telephonic communications should identify the subject matter of the communication and name the counterparty to it.
- CMC: Cross Matter Churning | Timekeeper records time for handling substantially the same task in the same time frame in two or more separate matters or invoices.
- ER: Excessive Research | Legal research occurs in an hourly amount in excess of client's guidelines for pre-approval.
- ET: Excessive Time | Time for a repeated or routine task (e.g., first draft of form lease for multi-unit residential apartments, draft of UCC-1 ) exceeds industry norms for similarly experienced timekeepers.
- ETD: Embedded Time Discrepancy | Discrepancies found with time notations embedded in line narratives.

- ETD-UB: Embedded Time Discrepancy - Under Billing | The hours billed for the line is less than the total of embedded time in the narrative.
- GRZ: Grazing | Senior timekeepers record hours when "shadowing" another partner or keeping abreast of matters without incremental value add to a client.
